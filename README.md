# Hi there, I'm Walid Ben 👋

Founder & Original Owner of **[EPUB Inspector](https://epubinspector.online)** — an independent, privacy-first web suite of browser-based ebook engineering, validation, repair, and editing tools.

---

### 🧑‍💻 About Me & My Work

I build client-side, browser-native tools that help independent authors, digital publishers, and ebook developers inspect, repair, and produce standards-compliant EPUB 3 publications without uploading sensitive manuscripts to third-party servers.

My work tracks:
- **W3C EPUB 3.3 & EPUB Accessibility 1.1** specification standards.
- Ingestion guidelines and formatting quirks across **Amazon KDP**, **Apple Books**, **Kobo Writing Life**, and **Google Play Books**.
- High-performance, client-side ebook processing running on WebAssembly and modern browser standards.

### 🛠️ Interactive Web Tools (100% Client-Side & Free)

Direct access to production tools hosted on [EPUB Inspector](https://epubinspector.online):

#### 🔍 Analyze, Validate & Inspect
- **[EPUB Validator](https://epubinspector.online/epub-validator)** — Validate EPUB packaging, manifest completeness, and structural integrity against retailer standards.
- **[EPUB Reader & Viewer](https://epubinspector.online/epub-viewer)** — Clean in-browser EPUB reader with full-text search, TOC navigation, and typography controls.
- **[Metadata Inspector](https://epubinspector.online/metadata-inspector)** — Extract and review OPF metadata (`dc:title`, `dc:creator`, ISBN, language, rights).
- **[EPUB Quality Score](https://epubinspector.online/quality-score)** — Automated 0–100 quality scoring based on structural health and accessibility criteria.
- **[Word Counter & Statistics](https://epubinspector.online/statistics-analyzer)** — Granular word, character, chapter, and asset counts.
- **[Structure Explorer](https://epubinspector.online/structure-explorer)** — Interactive visual tree of the publication spine, landmarks, and navigation.
- **[Asset Inspector](https://epubinspector.online/asset-inspector)** — Full inventory of bundled fonts, stylesheets, images, and HTML files with compression breakdown.
- **[Accessibility Checker](https://epubinspector.online/accessibility-checker)** — Audit accessibility tags, contrast, semantics, and WCAG/EPUB A11y conformance.

#### ⚡ Edit & Repair
- **[EPUB Fixer](https://epubinspector.online/epub-fixer)** — Automated repair engine for broken archives, missing manifests, encoding issues, and corrupted containers.
- **[Online EPUB Editor](https://epubinspector.online/epub-editor)** — Comprehensive in-browser editor to update chapters, metadata, and styling with instant preview.
- **[Table of Contents Generator](https://epubinspector.online/epub-toc-generator)** — Automatically scan headings to generate clean `nav.xhtml` and `toc.ncx` navigation.
- **[TOC Editor](https://epubinspector.online/edit-toc)** — Manually restructure, rename, or reorder table of contents hierarchies.
- **[OPF Package Editor](https://epubinspector.online/edit-opf)** — Direct editing of `content.opf` metadata, spine, and manifest items.
- **[CSS Editor](https://epubinspector.online/edit-css)** & **[HTML Editor](https://epubinspector.online/edit-html)** — In-browser code editing tailored for reading system compatibility.
- **[EPUB 2 to EPUB 3 Upgrader](https://epubinspector.online/epub-2-to-epub-3)** — Modernize legacy publications to EPUB 3 with semantic markup and HTML5 navigation.
- **[EPUB Splitter](https://epubinspector.online/epub-splitter)** & **[EPUB Merger](https://epubinspector.online/epub-merger)** — Split books into chapters or combine multi-volume collections into a single publication.

#### 🔄 Convert & Extract
- **[Word to EPUB 3](https://epubinspector.online/word-to-epub)** — Convert `.docx` manuscripts directly to compliant, reflowable EPUB 3.
- **[EPUB to Word](https://epubinspector.online/epub-to-word)** & **[EPUB to TXT](https://epubinspector.online/epub-to-txt)** — Extract clean manuscripts for editorial workflows.
- **[Cover Extractor](https://epubinspector.online/cover-extractor)** & **[Image Extractor](https://epubinspector.online/epub-image-extractor)** — Extract embedded cover artwork and illustrations in original lossless resolution.
- **[Chapter Extractor](https://epubinspector.online/epub-chapter-extractor)** — Export standalone sections as HTML or plain text.
